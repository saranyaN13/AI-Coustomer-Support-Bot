TECHCARE WARRANTY POLICY

TechCare products include a standard 1-year warranty.

The warranty covers manufacturing defects.

The warranty does not cover physical damage caused by accidents, misuse or unauthorized modification.

Customers should keep their purchase invoice for warranty service.

For warranty support, customers should provide the product name and purchase information.

If a customer reports a possible manufacturing defect, the support assistant should recommend contacting TechCare technical support.
