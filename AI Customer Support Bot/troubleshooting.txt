TECHCARE TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE

LAPTOP NOT TURNING ON

Check whether the charger is properly connected.

Try another compatible power outlet.

Make sure the charging indicator is working if the device has one.

If the laptop still does not turn on, contact TechCare technical support.


LAPTOP RUNNING SLOWLY

Restart the laptop.

Close unnecessary applications.

Check available storage space.

If the problem continues, contact technical support.


BLUETOOTH NOT CONNECTING

Make sure Bluetooth is enabled.

Keep the devices close together.

Remove the previous Bluetooth pairing.

Try pairing the devices again.

If the problem continues, contact technical support.


BUDS NOT CHARGING

Check whether the charging case has sufficient power.

Check whether the earbuds are correctly positioned in the charging case.

Try another compatible charging cable.

If the issue continues, contact technical support.


SMARTWATCH NOT CONNECTING

Make sure Bluetooth is enabled.

Keep the smartwatch close to the phone.

Restart Bluetooth and try connecting again.

If the problem continues, contact TechCare support.
